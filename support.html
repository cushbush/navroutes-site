Frequently asked questions

How do I optimise my route?

Enter the addresses you need to visit, add a Start and End where appropriate, then choose the route optimisation action. NavRoutes will calculate an ordered route through the eligible stops.

Why is my address not being recognised?

Check that the address is complete and accurate, including the postcode or ZIP code. Try a different format, select a more specific map result, and check your internet connection if the issue continues.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the app store account used for purchase. On iPhone, open Settings, select your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, and select NavRoutes. Android subscription billing is still being connected for the initial release.

Are my addresses stored or tracked?

Route history is designed to remain on your device. Address and route information may be sent to the relevant routing, map or address provider when you request optimisation or address services. Read the Privacy Notice for the full explanation.

Can navigation mark a stop complete automatically?

No. External navigation is a manual handoff. Return to NavRoutes and record the delivery outcome yourself.
