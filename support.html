Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

📌 How do I optimize my route?

Simply enter the addresses you need to visit, and NavRoutes will calculate the most efficient route.

📌 Why is my address not being recognized?

Ensure that you enter a complete and accurate address. If the issue persists, check your internet connection or try a different format.

📌 How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. To cancel, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions and select NavRoutes.

📌 How do I contact support?

If you need further assistance, please reach out to our support team via email:

  • Email: support@navroutes.com
  • Business Hours: Monday-Friday, 9 AM-5 PM (UK Time, UTC/GMT in Winter, UTC+1 in Summer)

📌 Are my addresses stored or tracked?

No, NavRoutes does not store, track, or retain addresses. Your route history is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us.
