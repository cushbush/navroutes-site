THE FULL DELIVERY TOOLKIT

More than a route
on a map.

NavRoutes keeps the practical parts of a delivery round together, from the first address you add to the record you review afterwards.

Plan the whole round

Add a Start, ordered delivery stops and a configured End. Choose current location or a saved address, then optimise the sequence and re-optimise remaining work when the day changes.

AI-assisted address import

Scan order pages or choose a page image. On compatible iPhones, on-device AI extracts delivery addresses and associated details for you to review and add to the route.

See planned arrivals

Review route distance, driving time, planned arrival and departure at each stop, time allowed at the delivery, and the planned finish of the round.

Navigate your way

Follow spoken route-step guidance and rerouting in NavRoutes Drive, or open the next stop in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. Keep your route and delivery details close at hand.

Bring the parcel numbers too

AI-assisted order-page import can carry over clearly associated parcel IDs, recipient names, piece counts, weights and delivery instructions when those details appear in the document.

Parcel and delivery photos

Take a photo or choose one from your library. Keep parcel-reference images and delivery-proof photos in separate galleries, with the option to view, replace, reorder or delete them.

Adapt as your round changes

Complete a stop, record a failed attempt or defer a delivery for later. Add reasons and photos, then continue with a clear view of the work ahead.

Keep the record afterwards

Review saved routes, inspect stop outcomes and route totals, copy stops into a new run, and export route records as CSV or PDF where supported.

Set the round your way

Choose a navigation provider, vehicle and units, avoid tolls, set planned time at each stop, and keep global driver defaults separate from route-specific overrides.

Work in your language

Choose a supported app language for route planning, settings and delivery records. Keep your working day in familiar terms.

Return to an active round

Resume a saved route after leaving the app, returning from external navigation or reopening the app, with stop states and route settings preserved.

Start with a free route

Plan up to 10 addresses, including Start and End, before choosing a subscription for larger rounds. Review current plans and local prices in the iPhone app.

AI-assisted document extraction requires iOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available. Review extracted addresses and details before import.

Can I try NavRoutes for free?

Yes. Plan up to 10 addresses on iPhone, including Start and End. For larger rounds, explore the available subscriptions and current local pricing in the app.

How does AI address scanning work?

Scan an order page or choose a page image. NavRoutes recognises the text and uses Apple's on-device AI on compatible iPhones to extract delivery details. Review the suggested map matches and confirm your stops. AI-assisted extraction requires iOS 26 or later, an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone, Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available.

Can NavRoutes read parcel numbers from my paperwork?

On compatible iPhones, AI-assisted import can extract parcel or tracking IDs, recipient names, piece counts, weights and instructions when they are clearly associated with a delivery in the document. You review the details before importing, including different recipients at the same address.

Can I keep parcel photos and delivery proof together?

Yes. Each stop has separate parcel-reference and delivery-proof galleries. Take a photo or choose an image from your library, then view, replace, reorder or delete it as needed.
