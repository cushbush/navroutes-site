ONE DELIVERY DAY / FOUR MOMENTS

Built for what happens
between the addresses.

Good route planning is more than finding a sequence. It is knowing what is next, what belongs there and what to do when the plan changes.

01

Plan

Add stops by search, recent address, current location, camera or voice. Set Start and End, then optimise the route.

02

Prepare

Review planned arrivals, driving time, stop allowance and the finish before the round begins.

03

Deliver

Keep parcel references, recipients and driver notes beside the next stop while using your chosen navigator.

04

Record

Complete, fail, skip or defer a stop. Add reasons and typed photo evidence, then review the whole round afterwards.

GOOD TO KNOW

Clear about
the limits too.

Does NavRoutes automatically mark a delivery complete?

No. External navigation is a manual handoff. Return to NavRoutes and record the outcome yourself, so opening a map app is never mistaken for a completed delivery.

Is NavRoutes Drive available on Android?

Android’s initial release is external-navigation only, using supported Google Maps or Waze handoff. The embedded NavRoutes Drive experience is an iOS feature.

Are the product images screenshots?

No. The product views on this website are original illustrations that explain the workflow without exposing the actual app interface.
