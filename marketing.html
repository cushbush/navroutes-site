AI address and parcel extraction

Your paperwork has
the details. Bring them.

Less manual entry before you leave. More delivery information with you when you arrive.

Scan an order page or choose a page image. NavRoutes reads the text, then uses Apple's on-device AI on compatible iPhones to identify delivery addresses and the details that belong with them.

  • Recipient names and parcel or tracking IDs
  • Piece counts and reported weights
  • Delivery instructions, including building and unit details

Review the suggested map matches and confirm your deliveries. Different recipients at the same address can stay separate, so the right details follow the right stop.

AI-assisted extraction requires iOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available. Extracted details depend on the source document; review them before import.
